## National Cyber League, Fall 2026

As captain, I led my team to a perfect score of 3,000 out of 3,000, with every challenge in every category solved. In the Individual Game my best categories were Scanning & Reconnaissance (95th percentile), Forensics (94th) and Web Application Exploitation (94th). I also got full points in Open Source Intelligence and Log Analysis, had 100% accuracy in Network Traffic Analysis, and earned 14 CompTIA CEUs.

### Reflection

My favorite categories were Web Application Exploitation and Password Cracking. I liked that web app challenges made me look at a site the way an attacker would and find the gap the developer missed. Password cracking was satisfying in a different way: once you identify the hash type and choose the right approach, you can watch the plaintext passwords come back. Those were the challenges I kept going back to.

The Individual Game also showed me what I need to work on. My accuracy was lower in Enumeration & Exploitation and in Scanning & Reconnaissance, and I ran out of time before finishing everything in Web Application Exploitation and Network Traffic Analysis. Going forward I want to slow down, verify my answers before submitting them, and learn my tools well before I need them under pressure.

Being team captain was the best part of the season. I gave each person the categories that fit their strengths, kept everyone coordinated, and had us check each other's work. We went from good individual scores to a perfect team score, which says a lot about what a team can do when people communicate and trust each other.
